# Commercial Licensing

The Aegis AI Gateway codebase is available under a non-commercial,
source-available license by default.

Any commercial use requires a separate paid commercial license from:

Spacewhale Software, LLC

## What counts as commercial use

Commercial use includes (without limitation):

- use by a company or organization in internal operations;
- use in production systems serving customers or employees;
- SaaS, hosted services, paid APIs, or managed offerings;
- consulting/client-delivery use;
- redistribution in paid or revenue-generating products.

## How to request a commercial license

Contact Spacewhale Software, LLC to request terms and pricing.
